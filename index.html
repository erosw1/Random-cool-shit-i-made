1D Heat Conduction Simulator
Finite Difference Method for Transient Heat Transfer
Theory

1. The Heat Equation (Fourier's Law)

The one-dimensional transient heat conduction is governed by:

∂T/∂t = α · ∂²T/∂x²

Where:

  • T = Temperature (°C)
  • t = Time (s)
  • x = Spatial coordinate (m)
  • α = Thermal diffusivity (m²/s), defined as α = k/(ρ·cp)

2. Finite Difference Discretization

Using forward difference in time and central difference in space:

Tin+1 = Tin + r · [Ti+1n - 2·Tin + Ti-1n]

Where r = α·Δt/Δx² is the mesh Fourier number

3.Stability Condition

For numerical stability:

Δt ≤ Δx² / (2α)

This ensures that r ≤ 0.5, preventing numerical oscillations and instability.

4. Boundary Conditions

Fixed temperature conditions are applied at both ends:

  • T(x=0, t) = Tleft
  • T(x=L, t) = Tright
