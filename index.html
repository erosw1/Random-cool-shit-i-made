Material Science & Heat Transfer Theory

1. Governing Equation with Multi-Mode Heat Transfer

ρ·cp·∂T/∂t = k·∂²T/∂x² + qgen - h·As·(T - T∞) - ε·σ·As·(T⁴ - Tsur⁴)

Material Properties:

  • α = Thermal diffusivity (m²/s) = k/(ρ·cp)
  • k = Thermal conductivity (W/m·K) - measure of heat conduction capability
  • ρ = Density (kg/m³) - mass per unit volume
  • cp = Specific heat capacity (J/kg·K) - energy to raise 1kg by 1K
  • ε = Emissivity (0-1) - surface radiative efficiency

2. Heat Transfer Modes

  • Conduction: qcond = -k·A·∂T/∂x (Fourier's Law)
  • Convection: qconv = h·A·(Ts - T∞) (Newton's Law of Cooling)
  • Radiation: qrad = ε·σ·A·(Ts⁴ - Tsur⁴) (Stefan-Boltzmann Law)

3. Dimensionless Numbers

  • Biot Number (Bi): Bi = h·Lc/k - ratio of internal to external thermal resistance
  • Fourier Number (Fo): Fo = α·t/L² - dimensionless time
  • Péclet Number (Pe): Pe = Re·Pr - ratio of convective to conductive transport

4. Microstructural Effects

  • Grain Size: Affects phonon scattering and thermal conductivity
  • Phase Composition: Multi-phase materials have complex thermal behavior
  • Lattice Defects: Dislocations, vacancies reduce thermal conductivity
  • Anisotropy: Directional dependence of thermal properties
